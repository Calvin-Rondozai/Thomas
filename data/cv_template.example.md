STYLE NOTES for the AI writer (not part of the CV text itself):
- Section headers in capitals, short and scannable, no decorative characters.
- Bullet points start with a strong verb, one achievement/responsibility per line.
- Never use an em dash or en dash character - use a comma, period, regular hyphen, or
  a word like "at"/"for" instead, in both the CV and the cover letter.
- Keep the summary to 3-5 sentences. Keep each role to 3-5 bullets, the most relevant
  ones for the target job first.
- Tone: confident, factual, no exaggeration beyond what's in the candidate profile.
