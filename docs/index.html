Thomas

A personal, single-user job-search assistant. It scrapes Zimbabwean job boards, matches postings against one candidate's own profile, drafts a tailored CV and cover letter, and lets that one person review and send applications by chatting with it on WhatsApp.

This is a personal project, not a public product - it is not offered to or used by anyone other than its owner.

Source code: github.com/Calvin-Rondozai/Thomas
